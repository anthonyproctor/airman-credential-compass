How the ranking works

This is a content-based recommender. Each credential is described by a feature vector, your profile becomes a vector on the same features, and the base score is the cosine similarity between the two. Practical checks (experience prerequisites, cost against your budget after funding) then adjust that score.

Data checked 14 October 2026 from each issuer's official site (fees, prerequisites), the AF career field education and training plans, VA.gov, and the AF COOL fact sheet. AFSC-to-credential links marked "inferred" were not taken from AF COOL. Fees change; confirm on the issuer's page before you pay. AF COOL eligibility rules are summarized from published fact sheets; your base education office has the final word. Built with Claude (Anthropic) as a class prototype for BANA 6780, CU Denver.
