# Avionics research notes (2026-10-14)

Data file: avionics_data.json in this folder.

## Current USAF enlisted avionics AFSCs (source: DAFECD, 31 Oct 2025, opened)
- 2A0X1 Avionics Test Station, Components, and Electronic Warfare Systems (title changed 31 Oct 24; was "Avionics Backshop Test Station and Components / Electronic Warfare Systems" in the 2017 CFETP)
- 2A2X1 "MHU-139 Electrical, Environmental and Avionics Technician" (as printed; changed 30 Apr 24; 1/3/5 levels only)
- 2A3X4 Fighter Aircraft Integrated Avionics (A: A-10/U-2, B: F-15, C: F-16)
- 2A3X5 Advanced Fighter Aircraft Integrated Avionics (A: F-22, B: F-35; RPA dropped since the 2017 CFETP)
- 2A9X4 Heavy Aircraft Integrated Avionics (A: C4ISR Mission Systems, B: RPA MQ-1/MQ-9/RQ-4; changed 31 Oct 25)
- Not current: 2A3X1, 2A5X3, 2A8X1/2, 2A9X1/2/3, 2A2X2/3 (none appear in the DAFECD). 2A5X2 is current but is Helicopter/Tiltrotor Aircraft Maintenance, not avionics. 2A6X6 is Aircraft Electrical and Environmental Systems (adjacent).

## CFETP credential language
- The current CFETP PDFs on static.e-publishing.af.mil all returned HTTP 403, so the current-edition text is UNVERIFIED.
- Opened the superseded 5 Oct 2017 editions of 2A0X1X and 2A3X5X (mirror copies). Both carry the same para 9.2 boilerplate: AF-COOL "supports programs like Air Force Airframe and Power plant (A&P) Certification; CCAF Instructor Certification; CCAF ISD Certification; JSAMTCC; and Professional Manager Certification (PMC)."
- Neither 2017 edition names FCC GROL, NCATT, ETA or IPC. Every mapping to those credentials is "inferred".

## Fees verified
- FCC GROL: Elements 1+3 (47 CFR 13.201). Lifetime license (13.15). FCC fee $35 (47 CFR 1.1102). COLEM exams: ETA $50 for up to 2 elements, plus $25 each extra; NMEA $50 per element. Radar Endorsement = Element 8.
- NCATT (SpaceTEC price list): AET $175; AEIT, ANS, DNS, OCS, RCS $150 each; UAS $225; FOE $250; AAA $175; BACC $175.
- ETA: AVN $80 (4-year term, $25/yr maintenance); CETa $65.
- IPC CIS (J-STD-001, IPC-A-610): valid 2 years (IPC help center). No official IPC price. Training-center examples: Everett CC $895 (J-STD-001), $715 (A-610, listing cites the old rev E); Fife College GBP 775 / 625.
- FAA A&P eligibility: 14 CFR 65.77 (18 months for one rating, 30 months for both, or a Part 147 school).
